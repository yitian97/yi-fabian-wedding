Our story

Our story started in 2020 during COVID, when there was nothing else to do. We became running partners, hitting the trails together to escape the monotony of lockdown. What started as a way to stay active and sane during those challenging times slowly transformed into something more. Mile by mile, run by run, we discovered a connection that went far beyond the paths we shared. Running became our love language, and we realized we were meant to be together. After two years, we adopted our baby Pipita, expanding our little family. Now, we're excited to take the next step and celebrate our love with all of you.

Ugh... dress codes?

We want you to feel comfortable, happy, and yourself—whatever that means for you. There's no strict dress code, just come as you are and ready to celebrate with us ❤️

Ladies
Please kindly avoid white, china red (the bride's color), and black.
If you'd like to match the wedding theme colour (and look great in photos), we're going for warm, neutral pastel tones.

Gentlemen
Smart casual to semi-formal—anything you feel good and comfortable wearing is perfect.

Good to know
Nights can be cool in May—bring a light jacket.

The Ceremony Venue

The ceremony will be held at Linnaeus Pavilion inside Marimurtra Botanical Garden in Blanes.

How do I get there?

It's way easier than you think!

Getting to the Ceremony Venue from Barcelona

  • By Train: Take the R1 train (Rodalies de Catalunya) from Barcelona Sants or Plaça Catalunya to Blanes station (approximately 1.5 hours). Trains run frequently throughout the day. Tickets can be purchased directly at the station. From Blanes station, it's a short 15 min taxi ride to the venue.
    Check train schedules →
  • By Car: It is 1 hour drive from Barcelona to the venue. Take the AP-7 or C-32 highway towards Girona, then exit at Blanes. Parking is free near the entrance; the blue zone area is also free.
    Get directions →

Please enter through the event-exclusive entrance shown on the map below. Do not use the main entrance.

🚌 Shuttle Bus Service

We will provide a shuttle bus from Marimurtra Botanical Garden (ceremony venue) to the banquet venue after the ceremony.

There will be an extra shuttle bus to transfer guests from the banquet venue back to Barcelona at the end of the night. The metro service in Barcelona will function 24 hours that day.

We are arranging hotel options near the venue. Please indicate your interest in the RSVP.

Extra transportation after the wedding may be provided if necessary.
